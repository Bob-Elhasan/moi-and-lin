# Fix verification brief
Review only: no edits, no generation, no credits, no git.
Each sheet shows up to 4 fixes. Per fix, three photos left to right: REF (approved front shot of the look), OLD (the defective shot), NEW (the re-rolled replacement). Captions below: line 1 = fix number, row and slot; line 2 = what the colors and shoes must be; line 3 (red) = what was wrong with OLD.
Slots: 01 front, 02 three-quarter, 03 back, 04 editorial front, 05 editorial back. NEW must keep OLD's camera angle and pose type (slot 01: NEW replaces the front itself, so REF and NEW are the same file).
For each fix decide ACCEPT or REJECT for the NEW photo. REJECT if any of: top or bottom color wrong vs line 2 or vs REF; garment design differs from REF (length, collar, buttons, layers, print); shoes are sandals/heels/open-toe or clearly differ from REF; more than one person; broken anatomy; a different-looking woman or clearly different hair color vs REF; dark smoky eye makeup; the original defect in line 3 is still present; NEW is worse than OLD in any way. Slightly different hair length on back views is acceptable.
Write a JSON list to the output path given in your prompt: {"fix": <fix number>, "row": <row>, "slot": "<slot>", "verdict": "ACCEPT"|"REJECT", "reason": "<short reason, required for REJECT>"}. Cover every fix on every sheet in your list. Be strict and honest; report only what you see. End with counts.
